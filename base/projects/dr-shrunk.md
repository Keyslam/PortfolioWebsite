Dr. Shrunk is a little game I worked on during 2020's Global Game Jam.
I worked in a team of 5 people consisting of 3 programmers, an artist, and a game designer.

I focused soley on programming, working on the steering and navigation of the viruses, interaction with the cells, and making the powerups.

All in all it's a cool little game with fantastic graphics. It was really fun to create.
