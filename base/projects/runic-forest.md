Magical Resonance was made in 72 hours for Ludum Dare 39.
I worked together with my friend [&I](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC39w2MAztMDW2gh5NDEA7Ew) on this game.
He composed the music and made all the sound effects while I worked on the code and graphics.

It was made in LÖVE using a fair amount of libraries to assist in development.

The game has an incredible amount of content for such a short time frame: 5 spells. 4 enemies. A voiceover. Menu. Spellbook. Etc.

It also scored really quite well ending up as 108th in the Fun category and 149th Overall.
