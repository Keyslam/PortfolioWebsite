In Planet Coaster there's a trick to make rollercoasters _incredibly_ smooth.
Sadly. The trick takes an insane amount of effort, having to do an action for every section of rollercoaster 4 to 8 times.
This usually takes a few hours to do.

To save time I wrote a script with AutoHotKey that does the majority of the process for you.
All you have to do is track the rollercoaster with your mouse once. After that the tool with smooth it out for you.
