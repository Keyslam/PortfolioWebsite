Woordwies is the first game I worked on at my internship at Grendel Games.
The concept is simple: You have a grid of 16 letters and a word. You have to translate that word to a different language and input it in the grid.

It never got finished in my times at Grendel Games. I think this was due to slow communication from the customer.
So in the end I implemented the core game and the people at Grendel finisihed it up.
