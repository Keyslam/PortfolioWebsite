Keep it Lit was made for Ludum Dare 46. The theme was "Keeping it Alive".
I was the programmer in a team of 3 people consisting of a programmer, artist, and composer.

Our concept was that you're hosting a party that you have to "keep alive" by providing snacks, making sure music keeps playing, and just generally keep people happy.

We wanted to focus a bit on humor by adding weird music tracks and funy flavor messages with the ingame "Quacker" app.

We finished the game just before the deadline with all the core mechanics in place, but sadly it was not as fun as we hoped it would be.
To be honest. The scope was a little too big for a 3 day game jam. I think with some more mechanics, balancing and polish we could turn this into a really fun mini game.
